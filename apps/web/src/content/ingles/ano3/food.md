## Useful words

- **I like...** 😋 → Eu gosto de...
- **I don't like...** 🤢 → Eu não gosto de...
- **I'm hungry!** → Tenho fome!

> [!TIP] **Para saberes mais** 🌱 To ask a friend: *Do you **like** pizza?* 🍕 And foods can be countable or not: *an apple* 🍎 (one), but *rice* 🍚 e *water* 💧 don't have a plural — you can't say "two waters"! 😄
